# Abstract

**{{FABERGE_TITLE}}** (**{{FABERGE_SUBTITLE}}**), package
{{FABERGE_IDENTITY}} v{{FABERGE_VERSION}}, implements three investigative
disciplines under the mission codename **{{FABERGE_CODENAME}}**, carried by
{{FABERGE_NUM_PIPELINES}} deterministic pipelines: each discipline pairs a
headline detector with a depth module that reads a different observable of the
same synthetic scenario. The pairing buys independence of *observable*, not of
sample — and, as the varnish result below shows, a second observable is not
automatically a second confirmation.

First, artifact forgery detection fingerprints painted objects by their
combined spectral reflectance and UV fluorescence. A ridge-regularized
non-negative least-squares fit identifies each object's pigment composition;
whether any pigment post-dates the object's claimed era, how strongly the
unexplained spectral residue resembles an anachronistic pigment (matched
filter), and the UV fluorescence reading jointly yield a forgery disposition.
Against a deterministic exhibit of {{FABERGE_NUM_ARTIFACTS}} artifacts drawn
from a library of {{FABERGE_NUM_PIGMENTS}} pigments,
{{FABERGE_SCREENED_ARTIFACTS}} objects are screened and
{{FABERGE_FLAGGED_FORGERY}} flagged as forged or likely-forged, with the
all-modern composition *{{FABERGE_FORGERY_WORST}}* carrying the highest score.
**Varnish-ageing kinetics** date two of those same artifacts from film
chemistry instead of composition, and the result is deliberately asymmetric.
The forger's fresh coat on *{{FABERGE_VARNISH_FAKE_ARTIFACT}}* is dated at
~{{FABERGE_VARNISH_FAKE_AGE}} years against a true
{{FABERGE_VARNISH_FAKE_TRUE_AGE}} and rejected as
{{FABERGE_VARNISH_FAKE_VERDICT}} in
{{FABERGE_VARNISH_FAKE_REJECTED_TRIALS}} of {{FABERGE_VARNISH_TRIALS}} noise
realizations. The century-old shellac on
*{{FABERGE_VARNISH_GENUINE_ARTIFACT}}*, claimed
{{FABERGE_VARNISH_CLAIMED}}, is dated at ~{{FABERGE_VARNISH_GENUINE_AGE}} years
against a true {{FABERGE_VARNISH_TRUE_AGE}} and returns a verdict of
{{FABERGE_VARNISH_GENUINE_VERDICT}} — but the same ensemble scatters by
{{FABERGE_VARNISH_GENUINE_STD}} years and yields
{{FABERGE_VARNISH_GENUINE_VERDICTS_SEEN}} distinct verdicts
({{FABERGE_VARNISH_GENUINE_VERDICT_SPREAD}}) from that one genuine object,
because the kinetics saturate. The channel rejects a fresh coat; it decides
nothing about an old one.

Second, circus-cover smuggling logistics route {{FABERGE_LOGISTICS_UNITS}}
payload units over a {{FABERGE_NUM_SEGMENTS}}-segment rail corridor as
minimum-cost flow via successive shortest paths; the resolved plan costs
{{FABERGE_LOGISTICS_COST}} unit-minutes and moves the payload with a makespan
of {{FABERGE_LOGISTICS_MAKESPAN}} minutes using {{FABERGE_LOGISTICS_TRAINS}}
circus trains, inside the stated capacity and the
{{FABERGE_LOGISTICS_DEADLINE}}-minute deadline. **Rolling-stock circulation**
then bounds the cover itself: {{FABERGE_ROLLING_TRIPS}} circus trips are
covered by a minimum of {{FABERGE_ROLLING_TRAINSETS}} trainsets (a matching of
{{FABERGE_ROLLING_MATCHING}} successor links, {{FABERGE_ROLLING_DEADHEAD}}
deadhead minutes), with a depot allocation costing
{{FABERGE_ROLLING_DEPOT_COST}} minutes. Two computed checks tie that schedule
to the routed plan: whether every routed leg is a scheduled trip
({{FABERGE_COVER_CORRIDOR_COVERED}}) and whether the fleet covers the trains
the flow needs ({{FABERGE_COVER_FLEET_SUFFICIENT}}).

Third, a concealed-device sweep over {{FABERGE_NUM_DETECTORS}} detectors fuses
a radiation anomaly map with shape/mass anomaly screening of
{{FABERGE_NUM_CONTAINERS}} cargo containers, flagging
{{FABERGE_SWEEP_FLAGGED}} and ranking {{FABERGE_SWEEP_WORST}} first.
**Radiation source localization** converts that alarm into a fix: weighted
Gauss–Newton estimation recovers the concealed source to within
{{FABERGE_SOURCE_ERROR_M}} metres of its true position, against a fitted
positional standard deviation of {{FABERGE_SOURCE_STD_M}} metres (residual
{{FABERGE_SOURCE_RESIDUAL}}, converged: {{FABERGE_SOURCE_CONVERGED}}).

Every pipeline is fully deterministic under a fixed seed ({{FABERGE_SEED}}),
producing reproducible, audit-ready provenance.
